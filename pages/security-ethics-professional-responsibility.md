### Case Study: The Equifax Data Breach (2017)

Because it is one of the largest credit agencies in the world, Equifax had a wealth of personal data. It was attacked in 2017, with data from around 147 million people being stolen. The cause was straightforward, Equifax was alerted to a critical vulnerability in one of its systems and was advised to apply a fix, but nobody verified that it had, and the attackers used that vulnerability to gain access, where they remained for months.

### The Impact on People, Companies and Society

* **People:** date of birth, home address and SSN were stolen. This is what criminals use to open accounts for identity theft, so people had to worry about fraud that occurred long after the breach and many needed to put their credit on hold or sign up for monitoring.
* **Consumer:** Equifax settled with US regulators and states for at least $575 million and was required to establish a security program audited by outside consultants. Its trust with consumers has been deeply affected as the company is seen as the guardian of their data.
* It proved that users can't decide if a company stores their data, and an error in one location impacts a significant part of a nation's population.

### Responsibility in Tech

All data held on IT systems should be protected by everyone who is responsible for designing and maintaining them. Equifax could have mitigated the potential impact by:

* Patching fast and verifying it was actually patched, not simply believing it was.
* Dividing up the network (segmentation) so if one breach occurs it can't spread everywhere.
* Not leaving admin passwords in clear text, and watching your systems so an attacker can't sit there unnoticed.

### Professional Behavior & Company Values

Being in IT is more than just technical skills. These are the behaviors that I think are most important:

* **Stay Calm Under Pressure:** Things won't always go according to plan or there will be delays, remain calm and have a plan of action.
* **Focused:** When there is a job to do, you are focused on it.
* **Values:** Always respect everyone and treat them well so you can build a good environment and always keep your comment to yourself.
