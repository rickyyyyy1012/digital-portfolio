### Careers and Companies That Interest Me
I want to start out as a network cable installer because I like the physical side of IT, moving around and working with hardware. It also seems interesting to me, and from there I might move on to more complex careers, but at the moment I have not decided. I have been looking at companies such as BT, Openreach and Virgin Media because they all frequently offer.

### How My Interests Have Grown
Before starting this course, my interests were focused on PCs, consoles and laptops, and fixing them when they had software or hardware issues. However, since starting this course I have become more interested in the networking side of IT because of my ESP lessons. Setting up and managing Windows Servers has been a fun and interesting experience, and it is leading me to look more into the network side of IT.

### My Technical Direction
The areas of technology I want to explore next are:
* **Learning how to make Ethernet cables:** I want to learn how these cables are made, as it would be good experience for a network cable installer job.
* **Setting up and configuring networks:** I want to learn more about networks, and I will do this in my ESP lessons by learning and using Cisco Packet Tracer.
