### Looking Back
I have enjoyed upgrading workstations in ESP lessons, setting up Windows Server os, configuring and setting up storage pools, Setting up a simple network, troubleshooting problems we had with the workstations. Over these weeks I have learned so much about networking and troubleshooting which has change my interest to a better path.

### What It Means to Be a Digital Professional
This year I learned that being a professional in IT is not just about Fixing hardware issues. It also means having knowledge in other sectors of IT, being confident in your actions, being good at troubleshooting, working with others, maintaining a healthy environment and being respectful to people around you.
